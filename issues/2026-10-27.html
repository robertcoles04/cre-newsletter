Trepp: prices steady, credit under pressure

Trepp and Commercial Real Estate Direct's Q3 2026 Quarterly Data Review finds property prices stabilizing while credit conditions tighten again. The report tracks where market momentum is holding and where it is meeting resistance.

Why it matters: steadier prices give buyers and sellers a clearer floor, but tighter credit means refinancing a maturing loan still gets harder.

$600M twin towers pitched near Dallas's Katy Trail

Jonas Woods and the Ignite-Rebees founders proposed a $600 million two-tower project near the Katy Trail in Dallas. The plan adds a large mixed-use development to a popular stretch of the city.

Why it matters: a bet this big says developers still see tenant and buyer demand in the Sun Belt despite high construction costs.

Debt Markets

Fortune International Group and Château Group secured a $463 million construction loan from Madison Realty Capital for the St. Regis Residences in Sunny Isles Beach, Florida. The package refinances existing Bank OZK debt and funds two 62-story oceanfront towers. Commercial Observer

Why it matters: construction lenders will still write large checks for luxury condos in strong coastal markets, even with financing expensive.

A big stack of commercial mortgages is coming due, and investors tell CNBC they see a chance to buy loans and assets at a discount. The idea is that owners who cannot refinance will have to sell. CNBC

Why it matters: borrowers who cannot roll over their loans may be forced to sell, handing cash-rich funds cheaper entry points and squeezing current owners.

Single-asset, single-borrower deals made up 76 percent of 2026 private-label CMBS issuance through September, with money rotating out of office and retail into apartments and hotels. CMBS bundles mortgages into bonds sold to investors. Commercial Observer

Why it matters: when bond buyers prefer one large, well-known building over a pool of many loans, smaller and weaker properties find it harder to get financed.

Distress Watch: The Worldgate Centre in Herndon, Virginia sold for $28.2 million at a competitive foreclosure auction. The Business Journals

Term of the Day

Positive leverage

When a property's income yield is higher than its loan's interest rate, so borrowing lifts your return. Buy a building that yields 7 percent with a 5 percent loan, and every borrowed dollar earns the 2 point gap. When the loan costs more than the yield, debt instead drags returns down.
