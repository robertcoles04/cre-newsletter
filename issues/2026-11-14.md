## Week in Review

The Trepp CMBS special servicing rate rose 25 basis points to 11.74% in October 2026, the highest since December 2012. Special servicing is when a loan moves to a workout specialist because the borrower is in trouble. Lodging drove the jump, making up 37% of the $3.01 billion that transferred that month. [Trepp](https://www.trepp.com/trepptalk/cmbs-special-servicing-rate-rises-25-basis-points-to-11.74-in-october-2026)

## Market Spotlight

Data centers dominated the week. These are large buildings packed with computer servers that run AI and cloud services, and they need cheap land and heavy power. Developers pitched multibillion-dollar campuses across Texas, Arizona, and Massachusetts.

Who gains: landowners near power lines, construction firms, and local governments collecting new taxes. Who worries: neighbors facing noise and strain on the grid. The risk is concentration. Jensen Huang's exit from Nvidia shows how much the boom leans on a few chipmakers and tenants. [Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/national/data-center-development/nvidia-lost-its-dealmaker-6-ways-jensen-huang-exit-could-hit-data-centers-and-cre) If AI demand cools, half-built campuses could sit empty.

## Term of the Day

**Balloon payment:** a large lump sum of principal due when a loan matures. Say you borrow $10 million and only pay interest for 10 years. At the end, the full $10 million comes due at once. Borrowers usually refinance or sell to cover it, so a balloon is risky if rates have risen or lenders have pulled back.
