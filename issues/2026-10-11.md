## Careers Corner

Learn to read a debt schedule before your first real estate interview. A debt schedule is a simple table listing each loan on a property: its size, its interest rate, and the date it comes due. The date it comes due is called the maturity. Knowing when loans mature tells you when an owner must refinance or sell, which is often where the real risk and opportunity sit. In a case study, pointing to a near-term maturity shows you grasp how financing drives returns as much as rent does. It is a skill brokers, lenders, and analysts all respect.

## Term of the Day

**NOI (net operating income):** a property's rent and other income minus its operating costs, such as taxes, insurance, and maintenance, but before any loan payments. If a building earns 1,000,000 dollars in rent and spends 400,000 dollars to run, its NOI is 600,000 dollars. Buyers and lenders use NOI to judge what a property is worth.
