## Term of the Day

**Bad-boy carve-outs:** most commercial mortgages are non-recourse, meaning the lender can take the building if the loan fails but cannot chase the borrower's own money. Carve-outs are the exceptions. If the borrower commits fraud, hides rent, or files a bad-faith bankruptcy, the loan turns personal and the borrower can owe the shortfall directly.
