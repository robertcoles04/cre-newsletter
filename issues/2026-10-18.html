Careers Corner

When a mortgage REIT or property lender reports earnings, read past the headline number and look at its loan book. Check how many loans are current, how many are in trouble, and what kind of property backs them. That tells you where the lender sees risk and which markets it still trusts. For a student heading into real estate finance, learning to read a lender's quarterly report is a free skill to practice. It also gives you something concrete to discuss with anyone working in debt markets.

Term of the Day

CMBS

Commercial mortgage-backed securities. A bank bundles many property loans together, then sells slices of that pool to bond investors as securities. The investors collect the borrowers' loan payments. It lets lenders free up cash to make new loans, and it spreads property risk across many buyers.
