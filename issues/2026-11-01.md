## Term of the Day

**Core-plus:** a property that is mostly leased and steady but has room for small improvements. An investor might buy a building that is, say, 85 percent occupied, spend a little to update units, and lease up the rest. The reward is a bit more return than a fully stabilized asset, with a bit more risk.
