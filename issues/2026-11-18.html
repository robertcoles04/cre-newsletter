Top Story

Service Properties Trust turns down a $2 billion buyout

Service Properties Trust, a hotel-focused real estate investment trust, passed on a $2 billion all-cash offer to take the company private, per The Real Deal. The board decided the price did not reflect what it believes its properties are worth.

Source: The Real Deal: Service Properties Trust turns down a $2 billion buyout

Why it matters When a seller walks away from cash, it signals owners and buyers still disagree on value, which keeps deals from closing across the hotel sector.

Top Stories

Loudoun County freezes new data center approvals

Loudoun County, Virginia, home to the world's largest cluster of data centers, approved a 12-month pause on entitling new data centers and electrical substations. The Board of Supervisors voted 7-1 to stop considering applications while it writes tighter rules.

Why it matters: Developers who counted on building in the busiest market in the country now face a year of waiting. That could push new projects, and the power demand behind them, into other counties.

Source: Commercial Observer: Loudoun County freezes new data center approvals

Blue Owl pays $780M for a Northern Virginia data center

A Blue Owl Capital affiliate bought a 54-megawatt data center in Gainesville, Virginia, for $779.5 million. The 363,220-square-foot building is fully leased to a single hyperscale tenant that was not named.

Why it matters: Investors are still paying a premium for already-leased data centers, so owners of standing facilities gain as a nearby county halts new supply and makes existing space scarcer.

Source: Commercial Observer: Blue Owl pays $780M for a Northern Virginia data center

Related and Kushner launch a $1B Jersey City project

Related, Kushner and RWJBarnabas are launching a $1 billion redevelopment in Jersey City, New Jersey, per Multi-Housing News. The partners plan to remake land around the Jersey City Medical Center campus into housing, retail and public space.

Why it matters: A billion-dollar bet on new apartments and retail signals developers still see demand across the Hudson from Manhattan. It also gives the city a major tax base and construction jobs.

Source: Multi-Housing News: Related and Kushner launch a $1B Jersey City project

Debt Markets

For three years, the fix for a troubled commercial mortgage was simple: extend the maturity and wait for rates to drop. Trepp reports that trade is breaking down, with loan modifications down to 37% of balances leaving special servicing in 2026 from 54% in 2024, while foreclosures climb. Trepp Why it matters: Special servicing is where distressed loans go to be worked out, and fewer extensions means more owners lose buildings instead of buying time.

The October inflation report offered little relief for commercial property borrowers, according to CoStar, with price data firm enough to keep the Fed cautious. Borrowers hoping cheaper debt would arrive soon got little new reason for optimism. CoStar Why it matters: When inflation stays sticky, the Fed keeps rates high, and every borrower facing a 2027 maturity pays more to refinance.

Casino operator Bally's lined up $560 million to ease financial pressure and push forward its Chicago casino buildout, per The Real Deal. The financing buys the company room as it funds a large project. The Real Deal Why it matters: Fresh capital keeps a half-built project alive, protecting the contractors, lenders and the city counting on the finished casino.

A GlobeSt report says the "extend and pretend" strategy is starting to fail as more CMBS (commercial mortgage-backed securities, bundles of property loans sold to investors) loans hit their due dates. Lenders had been quietly extending these loans rather than forcing a sale or default, betting that rates and property values would recover first. That bet is now coming up short as the bills come due, according to GlobeSt.com.

Why it matters: When lenders can no longer delay, owners who cannot repay or refinance may have to sell at a loss, which sets lower price comps that drag down nearby property values.

Distress Watch: Kushner and RFR face foreclosure at their Dumbo Heights office campus in Brooklyn, The Real Deal reports, as the owners struggle with the debt on the property. The Real Deal

Market Watch

Sun Belt

Key International wants to redevelop a historic oceanfront hotel on Ocean Drive into luxury condos, per Commercial Observer. The plan would replace the 10-story Marriott Stanton South Beach at 161 Ocean Drive, on a 1.5-acre parcel in Miami Beach, with a 13-story condo building. Commercial Observer Why it matters: Converting a hotel into for-sale condos means the owner expects buyers to pay more for homes than guests pay for rooms. It reads as a bet on how hot Miami Beach's luxury market still is.

West Coast

A Detroit riverfront complex sold for $83 million, the city's most expensive multifamily deal in at least a decade, per Crain's Detroit. The sale sets a new high-water mark for apartment prices in the market. Crain's Detroit Why it matters: A record price gives other apartment owners in the city a fresh comp, a recent sale they can point to when they refinance or sell.

International

Global Emission Systems, a Canadian manufacturer, is building a 135,000-square-foot headquarters in Troutman, north of Charlotte, per Connect CRE. The project is expected to cost $35 million, built on land next to the company's existing facility. Connect CRE Why it matters: A foreign company planting its HQ in the Charlotte region means local landlords and the town gain jobs and a long-term industrial tenant.

Term of the Day

Refinancing gap

The shortfall when a new loan comes in smaller than the maturing loan it replaces. If a building's value or income has fallen, the lender will lend less, say $7 million against an old $10 million loan. The owner must then bring the missing cash to close, or risk losing the property.
