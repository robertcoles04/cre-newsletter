Top Story

Cushman and Wakefield's Chicago HQ sells at a 45% discount

An affiliate of Prime Finance, in a joint venture with Hines, bought the 31-story tower at 225 W. Wacker Drive, which houses Cushman and Wakefield's global headquarters. The sale closed at a 45% discount to the building's prior value.

Source: Bisnow: Cushman and Wakefield's Chicago HQ sells at a 45% discount

Why it matters A deep markdown on a trophy Chicago office gives lenders a brutal benchmark for what similar towers are worth, pressuring refinancings across the Loop.

Top Stories

Democrats flip Congress as housing moves to the center

Democrats flipped both houses of Congress on Tuesday, winning seats in deep-red parts of the South, Midwest and Great Plains as voters revolted against rising costs. Housing affordability is now set to dominate the legislative agenda.

Why it matters: A new majority could push rent rules, tax credits and zoning incentives that reshape what gets built and who can afford it.

Source: Commercial Observer: Democrats flip Congress as housing moves to the center

Florida voters approve Amendment 3 property tax cuts

Florida voters approved Amendment 3, a far-reaching property tax cut, with 62 percent of the vote, clearing the 60 percent threshold needed to change the state constitution. Supporters say it will slash local government budgets.

Why it matters: Lower property taxes cut a major carrying cost for owners, but thinner local budgets could mean weaker services that eventually drag on property values.

Source: Commercial Observer: Florida voters approve Amendment 3 property tax cuts

Fort Point pays $440M for nearly 1,000 SF apartments

Fort Point paid $440 million for nearly 1,000 San Francisco apartments. The purchase lands as the city's housing market tries to find a floor.

Why it matters: A large buyer betting this much on San Francisco rentals signals that investors think apartment values there have bottomed.

Source: The Real Deal: Fort Point pays $440M for nearly 1,000 SF apartments

Push to end Fannie and Freddie conservatorship gains steam

A post-election push to end the conservatorship of Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac, the government-backed firms that stand behind most US mortgages, is gaining momentum. The two have operated under federal control since the last financial crisis.

Why it matters: How these firms are released could change mortgage costs and multifamily lending terms for nearly every borrower.

Source: GlobeSt.com: Push to end Fannie and Freddie conservatorship gains steam

Debt Markets

Refinancing costs for apartment owners have jumped 41% as a wave of loans comes due, according to GlobeSt. Owners who borrowed cheaply years ago now face far larger payments to replace that debt. GlobeSt.com

Why it matters: Borrowers who cannot cover the higher payments may be forced to sell or default, feeding more distress.

At Commercial Observer's fall finance forum on Election Day, major players described a calm before the storm, warning that the near future holds plenty of distress to work through. Commercial Observer

Why it matters: When lenders and investors openly expect more defaults, credit tightens and discounted buying opportunities start to appear.

A lender has filed to foreclose on a $236M loan tied to 35 West Wacker, a Chicago office tower, according to a new suit. Foreclosure means the lender is moving to seize the building after the borrower allegedly failed to meet the loan's terms. The filing adds to the list of large downtown office loans now in trouble. The Real Deal.

Why it matters: A foreclosure on a loan this size signals that the lender sees little chance of being repaid, a warning sign for anyone holding debt on older downtown office towers.

Term of the Day

Opportunistic

The highest-risk real estate strategy. Investors buy properties that need major work (ground-up development, distressed assets, or a full repositioning) and accept the chance of loss in exchange for the biggest potential returns. Gutting a vacant office to convert it into apartments is a classic opportunistic play.
