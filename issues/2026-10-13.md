## Term of the Day

**GP/LP:** two roles in a real estate deal. The general partner (GP) finds, buys and runs the property, and puts in a small slice of the cash. Limited partners (LPs) are passive investors who supply most of the money and share the profits, but do not manage anything. For example, a GP might put in $1M and raise $9M from LPs on a $10M purchase.
