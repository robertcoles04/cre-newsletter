## Term of the Day

**Cash sweep:** when a borrower breaks a loan rule, the lender can grab the property's leftover cash after bills and use it to pay down the debt. Say a building throws off $100,000 a month in extra cash; a sweep sends that to the lender instead of the owner, until the loan is back in good standing.
