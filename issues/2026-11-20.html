SpaceX Pitches a $50 Billion AI Campus in Arizona

SpaceX has proposed a $50 billion AI supercomputing campus in Cochise County, Arizona, according to Real Estate Daily News. The plan would plant one of the country's largest computing sites in a rural border county.

Source: Real Estate Daily News: SpaceX Pitches a $50 Billion AI Campus in Arizona

Top Stories

Deal Activity Falls 14% Into Year-End

CRE transaction activity dropped 14% as the market braces for a cautious finish to 2026, GlobeSt reports. Fewer buyers and sellers are closing as both sides wait for clearer pricing.

Why it matters: When volume falls, brokers earn fewer fees and owners lack fresh comps, which makes it harder to value a building or refinance a loan.

Source: GlobeSt.com: Deal Activity Falls 14% Into Year-End

Anthropic's $81 Billion Debut Spills Into SF Real Estate

Anthropic's growth lifted the San Francisco office market before its roughly $81 billion public debut, and The Real Deal says the next effect may land in local housing. A flush, hiring AI employer draws workers who need places to live.

Why it matters: New high-paid tenants can push up apartment rents and home prices, helping landlords but squeezing everyone else competing for San Francisco housing.

Source: The Real Deal: Anthropic's $81 Billion Debut Spills Into SF Real Estate

Debt Markets

Blue Owl Capital is preparing a $513 million single-asset, single-borrower CMBS deal to finance a newly built Northern Virginia data center fully leased to Amazon. A single-asset CMBS bundles one property's loan into bonds sold to investors. Commercial Observer

Why it matters: Bond buyers are still funding data centers even as politicians push back, so lenders keep competing to finance the sector.

The $175.5 million loan on Atlanta's 191 Peachtree Tower moved to special servicing after Banyan Street Capital missed its maturity payment. Special servicing is where a troubled loan goes to be worked out or foreclosed. Bisnow

Why it matters: An owner who cannot repay or refinance at maturity signals that older office towers still struggle to find new loans.

Birch Group's Jersey City office debt was also sent to special servicing, another sign of stress in suburban and commuter-belt office buildings. The Real Deal

Why it matters: More office loans entering workout gives special servicers a heavier backlog, which slows every borrower's path to a deal.

The Real Deal walked through how it traced a web of connected deals tied to Chicago's multifamily distress. The report shows that troubled apartment loans in the city often link back to the same players and overlapping transactions. Mapping those ties helps explain why the distress keeps spreading across related properties, The Real Deal.

Why it matters: Multifamily distress (apartment loans the borrower is struggling to repay) that traces back to shared owners warns lenders that one failing deal can drag down others tied to the same hands.

Distress Watch: A Trepp study of CMBS loan modifications since 2022 found one in five came with a paydown, and those borrowers were half as likely to be back in trouble two years later. Trepp

Market Watch

Sun Belt

Tesla has leased 82,000 square feet in the former GM Innovation Center in North Austin, The Real Deal reports. The move fills space inside a large, well-known campus in one of the Sun Belt's busiest job markets. The Real Deal

Why it matters: A big-name tenant taking older space helps backfill offices that risk sitting empty, steadying values for nearby Austin landlords.

West Coast

Rithm is lining up a $280 million CMBS loan from Wells Fargo and JPMorgan against a San Francisco office portfolio, per Green Street News. A CMBS loan is funded by bonds sold to investors rather than held on a bank's books. Green Street News

Why it matters: Major banks willing to finance San Francisco offices again suggests lenders see a floor forming under the city's battered office market.

International

Mapletree, a Singapore-based developer, has broken ground on a 952,720-square-foot industrial project in Manalapan, New Jersey, near Exit 8 of the Turnpike. The two-building site is set for completion in early 2028. REBusiness Online

Why it matters: A foreign investor building large warehouses near New York signals continued confidence in U.S. logistics demand, a bet that helps local contractors and workers.

Term of the Day

Prepayment penalty

A fee a borrower pays for paying off a loan early. It protects the lender's expected interest income. For example, a landlord repaying a $10 million loan years ahead of schedule might owe a penalty so the lender is not left short. It can make refinancing into lower rates too costly to be worth it.
