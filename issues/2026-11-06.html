Top Story

Vivmark posts its first report card as the country's largest apartment landlord

Vivmark Residential, the REIT (a company that owns income property and trades like a stock) formed this year by the merger of AvalonBay and Equity Residential, reported earnings Thursday. The company called out the integration of the two firms and growth in its first quarterly report.

Source: Commercial Observer: Vivmark posts its first report card as the country's largest apartment landlord

AI firm Modal Labs takes 157K square feet at a Tribeca office tower

Modal Labs, an AI cloud infrastructure company, signed a 156,763-square-foot lease at Rudin's 32 Avenue of the Americas in Manhattan's Tribeca, per CBRE's October office report. The firm is relocating from Hudson Square.

Debt Markets

A wave of multifamily loans is coming due, with roughly $250 billion of apartment debt set to mature and need refinancing. Many of those loans were taken out at lower rates than borrowers face today. www.connectmoney.com

Why it matters: Owners who refinance into higher rates will owe more each month, which can wipe out the profit a building was supposed to throw off.

Blackstone agreed to sell a $760 million portfolio of U.S. commercial real estate loans to Prime Finance. The sale moves a batch of property debt from one large lender to another. IPE Real Assets

Why it matters: When a big owner sells loans, it frees up cash to make new ones, and the buyer is betting it can collect more than it paid.

The Midtown office tower at 245 Park Avenue sits behind a $2 billion CMBS refinancing. CMBS means the loan is bundled with others and sold to bond investors. PERE

Why it matters: A large office refinancing that gets done signals bond buyers will still fund trophy Manhattan towers, even as they shun weaker office.

Bank commercial real estate (CRE) lending picked up in the second quarter of 2026, with origination volume reaching $9.2 billion, up 29% from the first quarter and the highest since the third quarter of 2022. The share of CRE loans that are behind on payments, the noncurrent rate, fell to 1.42%, its lowest since the third quarter of 2023. Trepp frames this as a rate turn landing on bank credit that was already improving on its own. Trepp

Why it matters: Banks are lending more on commercial property again and fewer of those loans are falling behind, so owners who need to refinance should find banks more willing to say yes.

Distress Watch: A nearly century-old San Francisco office tower is at risk of foreclosure after its owners were served a notice of default, the first legal step a lender takes toward seizing a property. The Business Journals

Market Watch

Sun Belt

Dallas-Fort Worth kept the top spot for the best overall real estate prospects for the third year running, according to the Emerging Trends in Real Estate 2027 report from ULI and PwC. Houston held steady at No. 6, while Austin slipped on its ongoing struggles. REBusiness Online

Why it matters: When investors rank a metro highest, more money and new projects tend to follow, which helps local developers raise cash and land tenants.

West Coast

Leasing by advanced manufacturing and robotics companies is booming in Silicon Valley, filling industrial buildings in the region. The demand reflects firms that need space to build physical products, not just write software. GlobeSt.com

Why it matters: New industrial tenants backfill space that goes empty when traditional users leave, giving landlords in a pricey market steadier rent.

International

Gencom bought the Rosewood London hotel in a deal valued at £300 million, with £225 million of it financed by debt, according to Bisnow's London deal sheet. The purchase adds a luxury London hotel to the firm's portfolio. Bisnow

Why it matters: A large financed hotel buy shows lenders are still willing to fund trophy hospitality deals in London, which sets a pricing comp for other owners.

Term of the Day

Non-recourse

A loan where the lender can only take the pledged property if the borrower defaults, not the borrower's other assets. Say a sponsor borrows $10 million against an office building. If the deal fails, the lender gets the building, but cannot chase the sponsor's cash or other properties.
