Top Story

CBRE: Q3 investment volume jumps 98% to $257.8B

CBRE, the world's largest commercial real estate brokerage, said global investment volume reached $257.8 billion in the third quarter, up 98% from a year earlier. Investment volume is the total dollars spent buying income-producing property. Yahoo Finance

Why it matters A near doubling of deal volume tells sellers that buyers are back, which can firm up prices after a long stretch of thin trading.

Top Stories

Industrial vacancy falls for the first time since 2022

Warehouse vacancy, the share of industrial space sitting empty, fell for the first time since 2022, GlobeSt reported, with newer buildings leading the recovery. Older warehouses with lower ceilings and fewer loading docks are still lagging. GlobeSt.com

Why it matters: Falling vacancy lets industrial landlords push rents again, and it signals that the glut of new warehouse supply is finally being absorbed.

Blackstone seeks $3B for real estate secondaries fund

Blackstone, the largest manager of private real estate, is raising $3 billion for a secondaries fund, The Real Deal reported. Secondaries funds buy existing stakes from investors who want to cash out of property funds before those funds wind down. The Real Deal

Why it matters: A big secondaries pool gives stuck investors a way out, and it sets price marks for fund stakes that had been hard to value.

Debt Markets

Trepp counts $4.42 billion of private-label CMBS debt reaching its hard maturity, the date a loan must be paid in full, this November across 182 whole loans. More than half of that balance carries a debt yield below 8%, a thin cushion that makes refinancing at the same size tough. Trepp

Why it matters: Owners who cannot refinance the full balance must add cash or sell, which can push distressed buildings onto the market at lower prices.

Even with borrowing costs climbing, CRE lenders are putting out more loan quotes, GlobeSt reported, a sign that capital is cautiously returning to deals. Lenders quote when they want to compete for business. GlobeSt.com

Why it matters: More quotes give borrowers more options, which can slow the rise in spreads lenders charge over the base rate.

The share of office loans in commercial mortgage-backed securities (CMBS) that are behind on payments has hit an all-time high. Many of these loans are coming due at a time when rates are higher than when they were first made, so borrowers who need to refinance face steeper costs. Older office buildings with weak occupancy are driving the trend. Green Street News.

Why it matters: When these loans go delinquent, the investors who bought the bonds can lose money, which makes them charge more to fund future office deals.

Distress Watch: CIM Group faces a foreclosure threat at 1440 Broadway, a Manhattan office tower, as its lender moves to take the building, The Real Deal reported. The Real Deal

Market Watch

Sun Belt

Jay Paul Company sealed $227.5 million of C-PACE debt to refinance a construction loan for the 881,427-square-foot Springdale Green office campus in Austin, Commercial Observer reported. C-PACE is financing repaid through a property tax assessment and used for energy upgrades. The deal is the largest C-PACE loan in Texas history. Commercial Observer

Why it matters: When conventional construction loans get expensive, owners turn to tools like C-PACE to refinance, which keeps troubled projects out of default.

West Coast

Active tenant demand across the big life science markets of Boston, the Bay Area and San Diego rose 62.6% from its 2025 low, and year-to-date leasing reached 6.9 million square feet, Newmark reported. Life science space is lab and research real estate. Vacancy is still high, but demand is turning up. Connect CRE

Why it matters: Rising tenant demand is an early signal that empty lab space will start filling, which helps landlords who overbuilt during the biotech boom.

International

Commercial real estate investment in India jumped 15%, Construction Week India reported, as global capital keeps flowing into the country's offices and warehouses. Investment measures dollars committed to buying income property. Construction Week India

Why it matters: As investors look past slower markets, India's pull shows where global money sees growth, pressuring other countries to compete for it.

Term of the Day

Exit cap rate

The cap rate an owner assumes when selling at the end of a hold. Cap rate is yearly income divided by price, so a higher exit cap means a lower sale price. If a building earns $1 million a year and you assume a 6% exit cap, you project selling it for about $16.7 million.
