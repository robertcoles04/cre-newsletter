## Term of the Day

**LTV (loan-to-value):** the loan amount divided by the property's value, shown as a percent. Borrow $700,000 against a $1,000,000 building and your LTV is 70 percent. Lower LTV means more of the owner's own cash is at stake, so the lender faces less risk if prices fall.
