## Careers Corner

When banks pull back, as the story on timid New York lenders shows, deals get financed by debt funds and insurers instead. Learn to tell these lenders apart. Pick one recent loan and ask who funded it: a regulated bank, a mortgage REIT, or a private debt fund. Each charges different rates and tolerates different risk. Knowing which lender fits which deal is the fastest way to sound useful in a meeting. It tells you who will actually say yes when a borrower needs money. ## Term of the Day

**Going-in cap rate:** a property's first-year net operating income (rent after operating costs, before loan payments) divided by the price paid. Buy a building for ten million that earns five hundred thousand, and your going-in cap rate is five percent. It tells a buyer the yearly return before any debt.
