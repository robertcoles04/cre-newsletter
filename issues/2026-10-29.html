Fed holds rates, but a December hike is in play

The Federal Reserve paused Wednesday, holding short-term rates six weeks after its first hike since 2023, in a 10-2 vote. Chair Kevin Warsh urged patience, pointing to last week's AI credit selloff, while the market braces for another hike before year-end.

Source: Commercial Observer: Fed holds rates, but a December hike is in play

Top Stories

JPMorgan's $6B tower raises valuation questions

An estimated $6 billion price tag on JPMorgan's new 270 Park Avenue headquarters in Manhattan is prompting questions about how to value a single office tower. The figure dwarfs what most offices fetch today.

Why it matters: When many older offices sell at steep discounts, a record cost for one trophy tower leaves appraisers and lenders without clean comparable sales. They have no easy way to judge what such a building is really worth.

Source: GlobeSt.com: JPMorgan's $6B tower raises valuation questions

Prosper approves $2B Venice-inspired community

The Texas town of Prosper greenlit a $2 billion, Venice-inspired master-planned community, though the project still faces major hurdles before anything gets built.

Why it matters: A large approval signals developers are still betting on Sun Belt growth. But the remaining obstacles show that financing and permitting now decide whether big plans ever break ground.

Source: The Real Deal: Prosper approves $2B Venice-inspired community

Term of the Day

Negative leverage

Borrowing to buy a property when the loan rate is higher than the property's yield (its yearly income divided by its price). Instead of lifting returns, the debt drags them down. If a building yields 5 percent a year but the loan costs 7 percent, each borrowed dollar earns less than it costs, so leverage hurts the buyer.
