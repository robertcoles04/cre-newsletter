## Careers Corner

This week's distress stories, from Texas foreclosures to Chicago workouts, are a free lesson in reading a troubled deal. When a property struggles, start with the capital stack: the layers of money in a deal, from the senior loan paid first to the equity paid last. Ask who holds each layer and who takes the loss if the building cannot cover its debt. Trace the lender, the borrower and any guarantees. If you can explain, in one sentence, why a specific deal failed and who absorbs the pain, you will sound sharper than most students in any interview.

## Term of the Day

**Yield maintenance:** a prepayment penalty that pays a lender the interest it would have collected if the borrower had kept the loan to maturity. Say a borrower wants to repay a 10-year loan early. Yield maintenance makes them cover the lost interest, so the lender earns its expected return either way.
